# Utopia Reworked

Modded version of cary's food chain simulator, which he demonstrated in his YouTube video! https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zy9N48udcdc

Note:  Processing 4 was used to make this simulator. Download Processing 4 here in order to run! https://processing.org/download

Controls: Press WASD + Space to move around. Press 'C' to have the camera start following the highlighted creature! (The one blinking white that is nearest to you.)
Press 'V' to change whether to control creatures or not, default turned off

Mutations have a 5% chance of appearing
Higher fertile lands = Faster plant growth, however lower fertile lands = slow plant growth
You can also play as the creatures, give it a try! (Except for the plants, but theres no reason to anyway)
I also split the predator creature into 2 different species that eats one type of both cows.
Cows and the predators can eat secondary foods that are not as appeasing to them, but they will prioritize primary foods first.
I might add more biodiversity in the future. (3rd food chain path inbetween mountain and lowlands? Maybe!) (Omnivores? Prehaps!) (Apex predator? Prehaps!) Let me know what I should add!
